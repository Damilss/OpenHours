# Product

**OpenHours** is an AI-powered office hours platform that allows professors to upload course materials and provides students with an AI assistant that answers questions strictly based on that content.

## Core Concept

OpenHours addresses inefficiency in education where professors answer repetitive questions and students wait days for responses. The AI is scoped strictly to professor-uploaded course content and guides students toward understanding rather than just giving answers.

## Target Users

- **Professors**: Upload course materials (PDFs, slides, lecture videos), view analytics on student struggles, manage office hours
- **Students**: Get instant AI help 24/7 scoped to their course, book office hours when they need more help

## Key Features

### For Professors
- Upload course materials (PDF, PPTX, lecture videos)
- Analytics dashboard showing what topics students struggle with most
- Manage office hours availability
- AI constrained to only uploaded course content

### For Students
- Ask questions and get AI-guided hints (not direct answers)
- AI responds only based on professor-uploaded material
- Book office hours directly when more help is needed
- Clean onboarding flow

## Design Principles

- **Not replacing professors** — augmenting their time, not their job
- **Scoped AI** — constrained strictly to course content (no hallucinations, no cheating shortcuts)
- **Hints not answers** — guides students toward understanding, preserves learning
- **Professor insights** — analytics on what topics students struggle with most
