# OpenHours Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenHours ("OpenHours," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use the OpenHours website, applications, APIs, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

OpenHours is an educational technology service that lets professors upload course materials and lets students ask course-scoped questions using an AI assistant. This Policy is designed to work alongside our Terms of Service. If a term is not defined here, it may be defined in the Terms of Service.

If you use OpenHours through a school, university, professor, or other educational institution, that institution may have its own privacy notices, contracts, policies, and legal obligations. In some cases, the institution is responsible for determining how student information is used, and OpenHours acts as a service provider or processor on the institution's behalf.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information created through your use of the Service, and limited technical information collected automatically.

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Name.
- Email address.
- Password credentials, which are handled by our authentication provider. We do not store your plaintext password.
- User role, such as student or professor.
- Account identifiers, authentication metadata, and account creation timestamps.

### 1.2 Course and Education Information

Depending on how you use OpenHours, we may collect:

- Course names, descriptions, identifiers, join codes, and course membership or enrollment data.
- Professor and student associations with a course.
- Uploaded course materials, such as PDFs, slide decks, lecture audio, lecture video, transcripts, file names, and extracted text.
- Document chunks, embeddings, and other derived representations used to search course materials.
- Course analytics, including question topics and trends.

### 1.3 Student Questions, Chat, and AI Interaction Data

When students use the AI assistant, we may collect:

- Questions, prompts, and messages submitted by students.
- AI-generated responses.
- Recent conversation history used to provide context for answers.
- Chat session titles, pinned status, timestamps, and course associations.
- Question logs used to provide professor analytics.

Students should not submit unnecessary personal information, highly sensitive information, health information, financial information, government identifiers, or other information that is not needed for a course question.

### 1.4 Professor Uploads and Administrative Actions

When professors use OpenHours, we may collect:

- Files uploaded for a course.
- Metadata about uploaded materials, such as file name, content type, source file, and upload time.
- Course creation, deletion, and management actions.
- Analytics requests and dashboard activity.

### 1.5 Office Hours Booking Information

If office hours booking features are enabled, we may collect booking requests, course identifiers, student identifiers, booking messages, statuses, and timestamps.

### 1.6 Communications

If you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, organization, message contents, and any other information you choose to provide.

### 1.7 Device, Usage, and Log Information

We and our service providers may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

- IP address.
- Browser type, device type, operating system, and language settings.
- Pages or features accessed.
- Referring pages.
- Dates and times of requests.
- Error logs, security logs, and diagnostic information.

We use this information to operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service.

### 1.8 Cookies and Similar Technologies

OpenHours and our service providers may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, preferences, and basic functionality. We do not use cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling certain cookies or local storage may prevent authentication or other parts of the Service from working correctly.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
- Create and authenticate accounts.
- Manage student and professor roles.
- Allow professors to create courses and upload course materials.
- Allow students to join courses and ask questions about course materials.
- Parse, transcribe, chunk, embed, retrieve, and search course materials.
- Generate AI-assisted responses scoped to course content.
- Save chat history and course-specific sessions.
- Provide course analytics to professors, including topics students are asking about.
- Process office hours booking requests if that feature is enabled.
- Communicate with users about accounts, security, support, and Service updates.
- Monitor, debug, and protect the Service.
- Prevent misuse, fraud, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Service.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce legal rights.

We do not use student personal information for targeted advertising.

## 3. AI Processing

OpenHours uses AI services to provide core product functionality. Depending on the feature, we may send the following information to AI service providers:

- Text extracted from uploaded course materials for embedding and retrieval.
- Student questions and recent conversation context.
- Relevant course material excerpts used to generate an answer.
- Question logs used to produce topic clusters for professor analytics.

AI outputs may be inaccurate or incomplete. OpenHours is intended to support learning and instruction, not to replace professors, institutional judgment, or independent verification.

We do not knowingly ask AI service providers to use student personal information for targeted advertising. Where applicable, we use service providers under contractual terms intended to limit their use of data to providing and securing their services.

## 4. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information as described below.

### 4.1 Educational Users Within a Course

OpenHours is built around courses. Depending on your role and course membership:

- Students may access courses they join and their own chat sessions and messages.
- Professors may access and manage their own courses, uploaded materials, and course-level analytics.
- Professors may receive analytics about student questions for their courses. Some questions may contain personal information if students include it.
- Course materials uploaded by professors may be made available to students in the course through the AI assistant or related course features.

### 4.2 Service Providers

We disclose information to vendors and service providers that help us operate the Service, such as:

- Authentication, database, storage, and backend infrastructure providers.
- AI, embedding, transcription, and analytics providers.
- Hosting and deployment providers.
- Security, logging, monitoring, and support providers.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us, comply with law, and protect their systems, subject to their contracts and policies.

### 4.3 School or Institutional Customers

If OpenHours is provided through an educational institution, we may disclose information to that institution, its authorized administrators, professors, instructors, or other personnel as needed to provide the Service, support educational purposes, comply with institutional instructions, or satisfy legal requirements.

### 4.4 Legal, Safety, and Compliance

We may disclose information if we believe doing so is reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law, legal process, or government requests.
- Enforce our Terms of Service or other agreements.
- Protect the rights, privacy, safety, or property of OpenHours, users, educational institutions, or others.
- Detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or technical issues.

### 4.5 Business Transfers

If OpenHours is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, personal information may be disclosed or transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections where required.

### 4.6 With Your Direction or Consent

We may disclose information when you direct us to do so or otherwise consent.

## 5. No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. We also do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are used under California privacy law.

If our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide any legally required opt-out mechanisms.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the Service.

Retention periods may vary by data type:

- Account information is generally retained while your account is active.
- Course materials are generally retained while the course or professor account remains active, unless deleted earlier.
- Chat sessions and messages are generally retained until deleted by the user or account owner, or as otherwise directed by an educational institution.
- Question logs and analytics may be retained to support professor insights and course improvement.
- Security and diagnostic logs are retained for a limited period appropriate for security, debugging, and compliance.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete, de-identify, or aggregate it, unless retention is required or permitted by law.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These safeguards may include authentication controls, database access controls, row-level access policies, encrypted transport, provider-managed infrastructure protections, monitoring, and limited access to production systems.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

- Delete personal information.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.

## 10. FERPA and Education Records

If OpenHours is used by an educational agency or institution subject to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA"), information submitted to OpenHours may include education records or personally identifiable information from education records.

Where required by FERPA and applicable agreements:

- OpenHours will use education records only to provide the Service, support the educational institution, maintain and improve the Service as permitted, comply with law, and fulfill authorized instructions.
- OpenHours will not use education records for targeted advertising.
- OpenHours will not re-disclose education records except as permitted by law, directed by the institution, or necessary to provide the Service through authorized service providers.
- OpenHours will use reasonable measures to protect education records.
- OpenHours will assist educational institutions with lawful access, correction, deletion, and inspection requests as required by applicable agreements and law.

Parents, guardians, and eligible students should contact their educational institution to exercise FERPA rights. If we receive a FERPA-related request directly, we may refer the requester to the relevant institution.
