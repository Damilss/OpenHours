# OpenHours Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026
Effective date: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenHours ("OpenHours," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use the OpenHours website, applications, APIs, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

OpenHours is an educational technology service that lets professors upload course materials and lets students ask course-scoped questions using an AI assistant. Answers are generated by retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over the materials a professor has uploaded for that course; the assistant is designed to guide rather than replace instruction. This Policy is designed to work alongside the OpenHours Terms of Service. If a term is not defined here, it may be defined in the Terms of Service.

OpenHours is operated jointly by its three maintainers — Emilio Scott, Colin Mcdonald, and Elias Santillan (collectively, the "Maintainers") — who own the Service in equal undivided shares and make material policy decisions, including changes to this Policy, only by unanimous consent.

If you use OpenHours through a school, university, professor, or other educational institution ("Institution"), that Institution may have its own privacy notices, contracts, policies, and legal obligations. In some cases, the Institution is the controller of student information and OpenHours acts as a service provider, processor, or "school official" on the Institution's behalf, in which case the Institution's notices and instructions take precedence to the extent of any conflict.

When OpenHours is used outside an Institution agreement, OpenHours acts as the controller of the personal information it collects.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information created through your use of the Service, and limited technical information collected automatically.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Name (full name, as provided at signup).
- Email address.
- Password credentials. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth, hashed at rest, and never visible to OpenHours in plaintext.
- User role, which is one of `professor` or `student`. Roles are stored in a `profiles` table that extends Supabase Auth and is created automatically on signup from `raw_user_meta_data`.
- Account identifiers, authentication metadata, email-verification status, and account creation timestamps.

### 2.2 Course and Education Information

Depending on how you use OpenHours, we may collect:

- Course names, descriptions, identifiers, and the professor-to-course relationship.
- Uploaded course materials, including PDFs (parsed with pypdf), slide decks (parsed with python-pptx), lecture audio or video (transcribed with the open-source openai-whisper library when enabled by the operator; otherwise rejected with a friendly error), the file name, content type, and upload time.
- Extracted text from those materials.
- Document chunks (approximately 500 tokens with 50-token overlap, produced by the LangChain text splitter) and 1,536-dimensional vector embeddings produced by the OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small` model. These embeddings are stored in the Supabase `documents` table using the pgvector extension and are used to retrieve the most relevant excerpts for a student question.
- Course-level analytics derived from student questions, including topic clusters generated from the most recent question logs (see Section 4).

### 2.3 Student Questions, Chat, and AI Interaction Data

When students use the AI assistant, we may collect:

- Questions, prompts, and messages submitted by the student.
- AI-generated responses returned by the OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini` model based on the top retrieved course excerpts.
- Conversation history within a chat session (`chat_sessions` and `chat_messages` tables), used to provide context for follow-up answers.
- Chat session titles, pinned status, timestamps, and the course associated with each session.
- A separate `question_logs` row for each question, used to power professor analytics. Question logs are scoped to a course and contain the question text, but are not displayed to other students.

Students should not submit personal information that is not needed to ask a course question. Do not submit highly sensitive information (such as health information, financial information, government identifiers, or precise location), confidential third-party information, or information about other identifiable individuals unless you have authority to do so and the submission is appropriate for the educational purpose.

### 2.4 Professor Uploads and Administrative Actions

When professors use OpenHours, we may collect:

- Files uploaded for a course (raw files in Supabase Storage; parsed text and embeddings in Postgres).
- Metadata about uploaded materials (file name, content type, source identifier, upload time).
- Course creation, deletion, and management actions.
- Analytics requests and dashboard activity.

### 2.5 Office Hours Booking Information (Inactive)

The codebase includes a `bookings` table and a `POST /book` endpoint that are not currently exposed in any user-facing interface. If a future version surfaces booking features, we may collect booking requests, course identifiers, student identifiers, booking messages, statuses, and timestamps, and we will update this Policy before doing so.

### 2.6 Communications

If you contact us — for example by emailing legal@openhours.me, privacy@openhours.me, security@openhours.me, or copyright@openhours.me — we may collect your name, email address, organization, message contents, attachments, and any other information you choose to provide.

### 2.7 Device, Usage, and Log Information

We and our service providers may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

We use this information to operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling certain cookies or local storage may prevent authentication or other parts of the Service from working correctly.

## 4. AI Processing

OpenHours uses AI services to provide core product functionality. The AI provider is OpenAI; the models used are `text-embedding-3-small` (embeddings) and `gpt-4o-mini` (chat completions and analytics topic clustering).

Depending on the feature, we may send the following information to OpenAI:

- Text extracted from uploaded course materials, for embedding only. Embeddings are stored in OpenHours's Supabase database; the source text remains in OpenHours's systems.
- Student questions and recent conversation context for the active chat session.
- Up to six retrieved excerpts from the course's `documents` table, used as the grounded context for an answer.
- Up to fifty (50) recent rows from the `question_logs` table for a course, used to produce topic clusters for the professor analytics dashboard.

OpenAI processes this information under its API terms. As of the effective date of this Policy, OpenAI has stated publicly that data submitted through its API is not used to train OpenAI's foundation models by default. We do not enable any opt-in arrangement that would change that default without updating this Policy.

AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. Embeddings are mathematical representations of text and, while not literal copies, can in some cases retain enough information to allow approximate reconstruction; we treat embeddings as personal information when they are derived from personal information. OpenHours is intended to support learning and instruction, not to replace professors, institutional judgment, or independent verification.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Default retention periods are summarized below; actual retention may be shorter or longer where required or permitted by law or by an Institution Agreement.

| Category | Default Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account profile (`profiles`) | While the account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion request |
| Courses and course memberships | While the owning professor's account is active or until the professor deletes the course |
| Uploaded course materials and embeddings (`documents`, Supabase Storage) | Until the professor deletes the material or the course; cascade-deleted when the parent course is deleted |
| Chat sessions and messages (`chat_sessions`, `chat_messages`) | Until the user deletes the session or the account; cascade-deleted with the session |
| Question logs (`question_logs`) | Up to 24 months by default to support analytics; may be deleted earlier on request |

| Communications with our team | Up to 24 months from the last response |

When information is no longer needed, we will delete, de-identify, or aggregate it, except where retention is required or permitted by law.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. Current safeguards include:

- HTTPS/TLS for transport between the browser, frontend, backend, and database.
- Authentication through Supabase Auth, with hashed password storage handled by the provider.
- Role-based access controls and Postgres row-level security (RLS) policies in Supabase, scoped to the authenticated user and their courses.
- Server-side use of the Supabase service role key only from the FastAPI backend, never exposed to the browser.
- Strict CORS allowlisting of frontend origins on the backend.
- Provider-managed infrastructure protections at Supabase, Vercel, and Railway, including encryption at rest of database and object storage.
- Limited Maintainer access to production credentials, with revocation on Maintainer departure under the License.
- Separation of duties between routine deployment access (any Maintainer) and Major Decisions about security posture (unanimous Maintainer consent).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

### 10.2 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account by emailing privacy@openhours.me. On a confirmed deletion request, we will (i) delete your `profiles` row, which cascade-deletes your owned courses (for professors) or your chat sessions and messages (for students), (ii) delete or de-identify your `question_logs` rows, and (iii) remove your raw uploaded files from Supabase Storage. Backups containing your data are overwritten on the regular backup cycle and are not separately searched.

### 10.3 Institution Requests

If you use OpenHours through an Institution, you may need to submit certain requests directly to that Institution. We will support the Institution as required by applicable law and our agreements.
