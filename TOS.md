## 6. AI Features

The Service uses AI systems provided by OpenAI to:

- Embed text extracted from course materials (`text-embedding-3-small`).
- Retrieve relevant course excerpts using cosine similarity over the pgvector index.
- Generate responses to student questions (`gpt-4o-mini`), grounded in the top retrieved excerpts.
- Cluster recent questions into topics for the professor analytics dashboard (`gpt-4o-mini`, capped at the most recent 50 questions per course).

You understand and agree that:
